Before you play

  1. On iPhone, search for your course and download it while online. No course is preinstalled.
  2. Select the downloaded course, choose one of its available tees, and start your round.
  3. Open Crane Course on your paired Apple Watch and check that the course and tee have synchronized.
  4. Allow location access for live yardages. Downloaded course maps, scores and GPS yardages can work offline; fresh wind estimates need internet.

Common questions

How does GPS work when the screen is off?

An open round keeps location updates active through phone locks and wrist lowers. Finish the round to save your scorecard and stop GPS. Finishing on either device also ends the round on its paired companion when it syncs. Returning to course selection stops GPS on iPhone. Location use can affect battery life. Old or inaccurate fixes are still hidden.

Why are yardages blank?

Playable yardages require a fresh, sufficiently accurate GPS fix near the selected course. Away from the course, the app shows an honest preview instead of misleading distances. Check location permissions in your device’s Settings, move outdoors and allow time for a new GPS fix. The Watch uses its own GPS.

Why can’t I download a course?

Downloads currently support the contiguous United States and depend on public scorecard and mapping data. Some courses do not have enough usable, numbered holes. Try the course name or city, check your connection, and retry later if a service is unavailable. Email us the course name and location if the problem continues.

What do the green distances and wind arrow mean?

Front, center and back are straight-line distances to mapped green positions. Center is not a live pin location. Wind is an Apple Weather estimate for the course area. Its arrow shows where wind is blowing relative to tee-to-green orientation, not the direction you are facing. Check local conditions and course signage.

How do I fix the wrong hole or synchronize my Watch?

Use the previous/next controls and manual progression when needed. Hole changes sync between your paired iPhone and Watch when connected. If a device is disconnected, reopen both apps nearby to receive the latest selection. For synchronization, keep the paired iPhone and Watch nearby, open both apps, and start the course and tee on iPhone. Confirm the Watch has received them before heading out.

Where are scores and downloaded courses stored?

They are stored on your devices. Round information synchronizes between your paired iPhone and Watch. There is no Crane Course account. Removing a downloaded course does not remove saved scorecards. Deleting the app can erase its local data; see the privacy policy for more detail.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone with iOS 17 or later, plus an optional Apple Watch with watchOS 10 or later. Set up the course and tee on iPhone before using the Watch companion.
