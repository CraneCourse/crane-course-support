Privacy policy

Effective October 20, 2026 · iPhone, Apple Watch, Android and Wear OS

Crane Course is developed by Eric Crane. This policy explains how the app and this support website handle information. Contact cranecourseapp@outlook.com with privacy questions or requests.

iPhone and Apple Watch

What stays on your devices

The app stores downloaded courses and aerial images, selected tees, scores, round history, preferences and cached wind locally. Round and course information synchronizes between your paired iPhone and Apple Watch using Apple’s paired-device synchronization services. Crane Course does not operate an account system or a server that receives your scorecards.

GPS positions used to calculate yardages and progress between holes are processed on your device. Crane Course does not upload a continuous location trail. Device backups may include app data according to your Apple settings.

Location and online requests

With your permission, the app uses location for green yardages, hole progression and nearby course search. During an open round, location updates may continue while the screen is off to keep yardages ready. Finish the round to save your scorecard and stop GPS. Finishing on either device also ends the round on its paired companion when it syncs. Returning to course selection stops GPS on iPhone. This does not upload a location trail. Near me filters our downloaded course catalog on your device; your nearby-search coordinates are not sent to the course directory. Course-name filtering also runs on your device. City search uses Apple Maps to resolve the city you enter.

Course search and downloadsThe app downloads the supported-course list and selected course files from GitHub Pages. GitHub receives ordinary request metadata and the requested file path, which can identify the selected course. Course-name and nearby filtering run locally. Apple Maps receives city-search queries.
Maps, imagery and elevationPublished course files derive from OpenStreetMap, OpenGolfAPI and Geofabrik. USDA NAIP supplies aerial imagery and USGS 3DEP supplies optional elevation. Imagery/elevation requests describe the selected course area, not your changing player GPS position.
WeatherApple WeatherKit receives the selected course’s center coordinate to provide wind estimates. The app does not send your live player position to WeatherKit.
Request metadataOnline services receive information needed to serve requests, such as your IP address, request parameters, app/client information and time. Providers may retain operational or security logs under their policies.

Network requests are not guaranteed to be anonymous: providers may receive IP addresses, request paths, search parameters where applicable, client information and timestamps. Each provider controls its own retention. See GitHub’s privacy statement, Apple’s privacy policy, USDA’s privacy policy, USGS privacy policies.

Android and Wear OS

Crane Course has no advertising or in-app analytics SDK, and we do not sell your personal data. You do not need a Crane Course account. Online features use outside services as described below.

Location during play

With your permission, Crane Course uses your phone's and watch's location to show yardages, help advance holes, and find nearby supported courses. These calculations run on the device. Crane Course does not send your live GPS fixes or a trail of your movements to our course, imagery, elevation, or weather services.

During active play, a foreground location service lets round GPS continue when the screen is off. You can manage location permissions in Android settings. Android and Google Play services also operate location features under their own settings and privacy policies, including Google's Location Accuracy service.

Course search, downloads, and weather

Course-name searches and nearby-course filtering use the catalog on your device. The catalog and selected course files are downloaded from our GitHub Pages site. City search looks up the place you type in a list of U.S. communities built into the app, from the U.S. Census Bureau and the U.S. Geological Survey; the text you type is not sent anywhere. Finding the courses near that place still needs an internet connection: the app downloads the course catalog from our GitHub Pages site and filters it on your device. That request does not include the place.

Course downloads request aerial imagery from USDA and elevation from USGS using the selected course's geographic area. The imagery service may use temporary cookies during a course download; the app keeps them in memory only. Wind requests go to the National Weather Service using the course's center, not your live GPS position.

These services retain operational request logs under their own policies: GitHub Pages, USDA, USGS, and National Weather Service.

These services receive network information such as your IP address, the requested file or geographic area, and an app client header. Requests can reveal which course you selected. Provider logs may include a general region inferred from your IP address; we do not promise that these requests are anonymous or immediately deleted.

Watch synchronization and saved data

Crane Course stores downloaded courses, maps, elevation, preferences, and round history in app-private storage. Active round information, scores, hole selection, and related settings synchronize between your phone and watch through Google's phone-and-watch synchronization services. A random device identifier helps keep paired-device updates in sync; it is not used for advertising.

Synchronization may use Bluetooth or Google's cloud relay. Google documents encrypted Bluetooth transport and end-to-end encrypted cloud transfers. Crane Course does not operate a server holding your scores or round history. Android or your device manufacturer may include eligible app data in backups or device transfers, depending on system settings.

You can remove saved courses and delete round history in the app. Deletions must reach the paired device to update its copy. The app retains deletion records to prevent removed rounds from returning during synchronization. Removing local data does not necessarily delete existing system backups or outside providers' logs.

Purchases and support

Google Play handles Android purchases and payment details; Crane Course does not receive your card details. If you email cranecourseapp@outlook.com, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. Please avoid sending unnecessary personal information. You can contact that address about privacy or deletion of support correspondence we hold; we cannot directly erase data held independently by Google or other service providers. We will explain what we can delete and any retention that applies to information we hold. For independently held information, use the provider's privacy controls or contact details. Google provides account data and privacy controls.

No advertising or tracking

Crane Course includes no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs, does not request an advertising identifier, and does not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising. The app sends no name, email address or account identifier with course searches. Because network providers receive IP addresses, their requests are not guaranteed to be unlinked to a person or device.

Support and purchases

If you email support, we receive your email address and the content and attachments you choose to send, through Outlook. We use them to answer your request and troubleshoot the app. We retain correspondence as needed to resolve the issue and maintain relevant support records; you can request deletion. Please avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information.

Apple handles App Store purchases and payment information. Crane Course does not collect your card or bank details. Apple may provide developers with sales reports and diagnostics under Apple’s settings and policies.

Your choices and deletion

  • Change or revoke location access and Precise Location in your device’s Settings. Live yardages need adequate location accuracy.
  • Course-name and nearby-course filtering happen on your device. On Apple apps, use course-name search if you do not want to send a city-search query to Apple Maps. Android City search does not send your query anywhere.
  • Remove downloaded courses in the app. Saved scorecards remain separate.
  • To remove locally stored app data, delete Crane Course from each device or use Android’s app-storage controls. Backup copies are managed through your Apple, Android or device-manufacturer backup settings. Reinstallation or device restoration may restore backed-up data.
  • Email cranecourseapp@outlook.com to request access, correction or deletion of information you have sent us. For provider-held request logs, the provider’s own policy and request process apply.

This website

This is a static support website. We add no analytics, advertising, forms, cookies or browser storage. GitHub Pages hosts this website. GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not they are signed in to GitHub. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. External links open services with their own privacy practices.

Children and changes

Crane Course is designed for teen and adult golfers and is not directed at children under 13. App-store age ratings describe content suitability. We do not knowingly solicit children’s personal information. Contact us if a child has sent personal information to support so we can address it.

We may update this policy when the app or its services change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
